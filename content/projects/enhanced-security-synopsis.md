problem:
  en: "Data security has become critical as companies depend on secure data flows, while data leaks and cybercrime are rising. The question was how encryption and access control can best protect data flows in networked systems and support GDPR compliance, and how to balance strong security against efficiency in Industry 4.0."

# Source: EXAM-DTI p.3 (Teoretisk grundlag: CIA-trekanten; Analyse: AES, RSA, RBAC, MFA,
# netværkssegmentering, fordele og ulemper), p.4 (Metode: kvalitativ tilgang, casestudier af ERP- og

method:
  en: "A qualitative analysis built on the CIA triad (confidentiality, integrity and availability), with case studies of ERP and CRM systems and secondary data from reports and literature. It compares symmetric (AES) and asymmetric (RSA) encryption, role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA) and network segmentation, weighs better security and GDPR compliance against cost, complexity and system speed, and puts the findings in perspective with examples from healthcare (Epic Systems) and the cloud (AWS)."
  da: "En kvalitativ analyse bygget på CIA-trekanten (fortrolighed, integritet og tilgængelighed) med casestudier af ERP- og CRM-systemer og sekundære data fra rapporter og litteratur. Den sammenligner symmetrisk (AES) og asymmetrisk (RSA) kryptering, rollebaseret adgangskontrol (RBAC), multifaktorgodkendelse (MFA) og netværkssegmentering, vejer bedre sikkerhed og GDPR-overholdelse op mod omkostninger, kompleksitet og systemhastighed og perspektiverer til eksempler fra sundhedssektoren (Epic Systems) og cloud (AWS)."
