problem:
  en: "Royal Unibrew's project culture is built around waterfall, while IT projects increasingly need agility, valid management data and transparency. ProjectFlow 365, Azure DevOps and Power BI were not integrated, and unclear roles and weak data discipline left the PMO without an overview of projects, resources and progress. The research question: how can Royal Unibrew strengthen its project management by developing a hybrid PMO model that combines structured governance, agile execution and data-driven management?"
